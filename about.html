Our Story

From One Building to a City-Shaping Vision

Xtreme Trade International started in 2008 with a single residential building in Uttara and a simple promise: never compromise on trust or quality.

Today, that promise has grown into 42 delivered projects, more than 2,500 happy families, and a pipeline that includes Bangladesh's most ambitious smart township. We remain proudly Bangladeshi — and relentlessly international in our standards.

  • Member of REHAB & FBCCI
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management
  • BCI-registered engineering team

Our Mission

To make world-class, safe and sustainable living accessible to every Bangladeshi family — through honest dealings, superior engineering and customer-first service at every step.
